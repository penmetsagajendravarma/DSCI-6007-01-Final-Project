Autocorrelation Function (ACF) & Partial Autocorrelation Function (PACF)
{% if results.plots.acf_pacf %}
What to Look For:
  • ACF: Measures correlation between the series and its lags. Gradual decay suggests AR process, sharp cutoffs suggest MA process.
  • PACF: Measures direct correlation between the series and its lags. Sharp cutoffs after lag p suggest AR(p) process.
  • Significant spikes at seasonal lags (e.g., 12 for monthly data) suggest seasonality.
